## ○‎‎ ‎ ‎‎ My Version naming
> [!WARNING]
> I use the same naming system or commits (almost).

I don't really care about the standards. My projects are often the projects of a passionate who discovered than you could burn a low-end PC if you "accidently" wrote `while True` in your python code. I wanted to have my own version naming to just make it clear for me, or for a team i could work with.
Maybe one day i will switch to "traditional version naming", but for now i will stick to this :

> [!WARNING]
> information* : non-optional
```
[prerelease]-[year*].[major*].[minor*]
```


`Pre-release - word` : <br>
> Optional, it usually is a greek letter symbolising an order of pre-release ("alpha", "beta"). <br>

`Year - Integer` : <br>
> Year the version has been **created** (not published). <br>

`Major - Integer` : <br>
> The order of a major update of the project. I consider a major as either a full remake of a section or the whole project, or it can be a new independant feature, wether it is small or big, because usually it means i will develop it even more, if i know it won't the case, then it is not a major update. <br>

`Minor - Letter` : <br>
> Well all the rest, sometimes, my stupid self will push few changes of words for some reasons.
